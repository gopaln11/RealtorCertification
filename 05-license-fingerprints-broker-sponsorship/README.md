# 5. License, fingerprints, and broker sponsorship

File only after all three courses are complete and you have official transcripts or completion certificates. One combined application covers the salesperson exam and the original license. File it once, in eLicensing. A duplicate online and mail application can charge you twice. Fees are not refundable (Business and Professions Code section 10207).

Cash to DRE for the combined filing, from the [fee page](materials/fees.md): salesperson examination $100, original salesperson license $350, combined total due to DRE $450. Re-examination $100. Rescheduled exam $40. Fingerprint processing $49 is paid at Live Scan if you live in California, plus the operator’s rolling fee. It is not part of the $450.

RE 206 (Rev. 7/26), saved here, prints a salesperson combined total of $499 because it adds that $49 fingerprint line into the sum. Footnote 1 on the same form says a California resident pays the $49 to the Live Scan provider, not to DRE. The same pattern appears on the original-license lines ($399 salesperson, $499 broker). For a California resident, pay DRE the fee-page total ($450 combined) and pay the fingerprint charges at Live Scan. Read the form and the fee page the day you pay.

You may be printed after you apply or after you pass. The processing fee is not refunded if you never pass. Practical choice: print after the application is in and you have an exam date. Take RE 237 to a Live Scan site. Locations: [oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/locations](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/locations). Send DRE the copy with Part 3 completed. The license is not issued until DOJ and FBI reports are received and screened. The fingerprint page says a Social Security number is required on the examination application so the reports match the file. The steps brochure also mentions an ITIN in its eligibility line. Follow the live application if those two wordings differ, and ask DRE before you guess.

After you pass, a license with no broker affiliation still does not allow licensed work. Add the responsible broker in eLicensing and have the broker, or the designated officer of a corporate broker, certify. The tutorial is saved here. If they will not use eLicensing, Salesperson Change Application RE 214 is the paper form. Only that broker may pay you for licensed work.

Mail backup for the combined application: Department of Real Estate, Examination Section, 651 Bannon Street, Suite 503, Sacramento, CA 95811, with RE 435 and the fee. Do not also file online. Do not send cash.

DRE processing time is posted weekly and is often longer than the department would like. The narrative page is saved. The weekly numbers were not, because the menu URL returned HTTP 404 and the current-timeframes page did not expose figures as text. Read [current processing times](https://www.dre.ca.gov/Licensees/CurrentTimeframes.html) the week you apply.

This is not legal advice. Roadmap: [README](../README.md). Working checklist: [california-practice-path.md](../docs/california-practice-path.md).

## What still has to be purchased

Nothing from a school at this step. You pay DRE and the Live Scan operator. eLicensing is the filing system, not a course.

Exam-only filing, if you ever split the exam from the license, is RE 400A. The path in the checklist is the combined RE 435, not RE 400A.

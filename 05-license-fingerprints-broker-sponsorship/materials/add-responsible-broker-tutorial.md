## eLicensing Tutorial: Add Responsible Broker

 Salesperson with an NBA (No Broker Affiliation) status can add a Responsible Broker
 using eLicensing. To do so:

- Sign in to eLicensing. If you have never used eLicensing, you will need to
 complete the registration step to create a user name and password. Thereafter, clicking on the eLicensing
 graphic and signing in by entering your user name and password will provide
 access to eLicensing. (elic_tutorial_register.html)

- Click on ADD RESPONSIBLE BROKER .

- Enter the LICENSE NUMBER of the new responsible broker or corporation.
 Click on DISPLAY BROKER INFORMATION.

- If the responsible broker information displayed is correct, click on SAVE
 BROKER INFORMATION.

- Your new (or existing) broker MUST certify your
 affiliation. eLicensing asks if the broker is available now to certify
 the affiliation. Click on YES or NO . If YES , eLicensing will display the responsible broker/corporation and
 asks the responsible broker to enter his/her username and password. After the
 responsible broker (or designated officer of the corporation, if applicable)
 enters his/her username and password, the responsible broker should click on CERTIFY . If NO , the responsible broker or designated broker/officer is not immediately available to certify your affiliation of the salesperson, you
 may enter his/her email address* and click on SAVE EMAIL . eLicensing will send an email to your responsible broker to advise that
 your affiliation needs to be certified. To certify your affiliation your
 responsible broker/designated officer of the corporation will need to sign in to
 eLicensing, click on the CERTIFY SALESPERSON AFFILIATION menu option,
 click in the box next to your name to place a check mark and click CERTIFY .

 * Please note, if the broker's
 email address is not entered or is mistyped, eLicensing does not allow you to go back
 to correct it. You will need to let your responsible broker know that your
 affiliation needs to be certified. 

- Click on FINISHED .

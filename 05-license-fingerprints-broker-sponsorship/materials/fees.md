### Examination Fees

| | Salesperson | Broker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original Examination | $100 | $150 |
| Re-Examination | $100 | $150 |
| First Rescheduled Exam | $40 | $45 |
| Subsequent Rescheduled Exam | $40 | $45 |

### Combination Examination and License Application

For those applicants who want to file their examination and license application at the same time. For more information,
 salesperson applicants may review Salesperson Exam/License Application
 (RE 435) (/files/pdf/forms/re435.pdf) and broker applicants may review the
 Broker Exam/License Application (RE 436) (/files/pdf/forms/re436.pdf)
.

Salesperson

| Examination Fee | $100 |
| --- | --- |
| License Fee | $350 |
| Fingerprint Fee | * |
| Total Due to DRE with RE 435 | $450 |

Broker

| Examination Fee | $150 |
| --- | --- |
| License Fee | $450 |
| Fingerprint Fee | * |
| Total Due to DRE with RE 436 | $600 |

* Original license or examination applicants who reside in California will pay a $49 fingerprint
 processing fee directly to the live scan fingerprint service provider. Original license applicants who
 reside out of state should submit the $49 fingerprint processing fee to the DRE with their application
 and license fee. See Fingerprint Requirements (Fingerprint.html) for further information.

### Corporation License Fees

When officer applicant is currently licensed by the DRE:

| License Fee | $450 |
| --- | --- |

When officer applicant has never been licensed by the DRE or applicant license expired more than two years ago:

| License Fee | $450 |
| --- | --- |
| Total Due to DRE | $450 |

When corporation has expired or when officer applicant's broker license has expired during the past two years:

| License Fee | $675 |
| --- | --- |

When the corporation is currently licensed and a substitution of a currently licensed additional officer is requested,
 no fee is required.

### Restricted License Fees

When a restricted license application must be submitted as a result of an Order following a hearing or other formal
 action:

| Salesperson License Fee | $350 |
| --- | --- |
| Broker License Fee | $450 |
| Officer License Fee | $450 |
| Corporation License Fee | $450 |

### Miscellaneous Fees

- Child Support Obligors : $95 (ChildSupport.html)

# 4. Salesperson exam prep

Not a statutory course. DRE does not require a prep class. You do have to pass the exam. Start timed drills as Practice is ending. Two to three weeks is enough if the three courses are actually finished.

Pass mark to use on practice sets: be steady at or above 80 percent on timed 150-question exams before you sit. The real passing line is 70 percent, 105 of 150, and a pass does not come with a score. The arithmetic is real-estate arithmetic. The risk is rusty hand calculation. [ref26.pdf](materials/ref26.pdf) is the 2010 book’s formula chapter. Practice every calculation by hand.

The [Taking the Exam](materials/taking-the-exam.md) page lists calculators among items that are not allowed in the room. Commissioner’s Regulation 2763, quoted on that same page, still describes a silent, non-programmable pocket calculator without a printer or alphabetic keyboard as something an examinee may use. Those two instructions conflict. On exam day, follow the schedule notice and the proctor.

Closest center to Irvine: DRE Electronic Exam Center, 1 Centerpointe Drive, Suite 370, 3rd Floor, La Palma, CA 90623. Walk-ins are not accepted. Results on the electronic exam are given when you finish. If you fail, the re-examination fee is $100. The form is RE 415A, saved here. There is no cap on attempts inside the two-year life of the original application.

This is not legal advice. Roadmap: [README](../README.md). Working checklist: [california-practice-path.md](../docs/california-practice-path.md).

## What still has to be purchased

A question bank, if you want one. The bank that comes with the school package, or a separate prep subscription, is commercial and was not downloaded. PrepAgent, Kaplan, The CE Shop, and OnlineEd materials are not in this folder. Official outlines are. Build one sheet of California traps for the last two weeks: deed of trust versus mortgage, when the agency disclosure is required, the Transfer Disclosure Statement and natural hazard disclosures, who may hold trust funds, who may pay a salesperson, what an unlicensed assistant may not do, advertising rules, community property and vesting, and proration, commission, area, interest, and loan amount by hand.

# La Palma Electronic Exam Center

| Exam Center Address: 1 Centerpointe Drive Suite 370, 3rd Floor La Palma, CA 90623 Location: Bounded by Valley View Street and Orangethorpe Avenue Exam Center Hours: Exams scheduled on an as-needed basis. Walk-ins are NOT allowed. Exam Room: Suite 370, 3rd Floor Additional Information: Arrive no more than 1/2 hour prior to exam. Acceptable Forms of Identification: To be admitted into an examination, you must show a valid form of photo identification. Only the following
 forms of photo identification will be accepted: Current state-issued driver's license or DMV identification card U.S. Passport or Passport issued by a foreign government U.S. Military identification card You will not be admitted into the examination without a valid form of photo identification as
 listed above. Section 10153.1 of the B&P Code states that it is unlawful for any person with respect to any examination
 under this part to practice any deception or fraud with regard to his or her identity in connection with any
 examination application, or request to be examined. Commissioners Regulation 2731(b) provides that applicants
 must provide their legal name when applying for a license. | Parking Information: Exam Center Parking is located at 8 Centerpointe Drive, on the corner of
 Valley View Street and Centerpointe Drive and is restricted to the top deck of the Parking Structure. Parking may be
 limited; arrive early. IMPORTANT: The Parking Permit included with your schedule MUST be displayed. Violators may be towed
 at owner's expense. (If you need a duplicate schedule notice, you can log on to your eLicensing account, visit Examination Services , select your exam date and print out a schedule notice.) Cell phone use and/or mere possession of a cell phone during an exam is strictly prohibited,
 including while out on break. All cell phones must be powered off and placed in an individual locker provided for your use. When individual
 lockers are unavailable, a Personal Belongings storage table will be set up for disallowed items. Failure to comply
 with examination administration rules will lead to your disqualification. You cannot inspect your book or answer sheet after the examination session has ended (Section 1798.40(e) of the
 California Civil Code). The Department of Real Estate and its employees will not be liable or responsible for the loss or damage of any personal
 belongings that are brought to a real estate examination. If personal belongings are brought to the examination, they
 are to be placed in a storage area apart from the owner, as directed by proctor staff. Be advised that DRE will not be responsible for lost or stolen items . Examinations are subject to cancellation. In the event that unforeseen circumstances force the closure of an exam
 center, your exam date will be rescheduled at no additional cost. The Department will not be held responsible for any
 expenses incurred beyond the cost of the exam, including but not limited to travel expenses and lost wages on the day
 of the exam. |

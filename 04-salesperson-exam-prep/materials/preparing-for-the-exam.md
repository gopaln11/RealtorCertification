# Preparing for the Exam

The laws administered by the Real Estate Commissioner, the Regulations of the Real Estate Commissioner, and extracts from other
 pertinent California codes are contained in the Real Estate Law (/Publications/RealEstateLaw.html). The Real Estate
 Law book is available on-line or the book and CD can be purchased at any DRE office (/Contact.html) or by submitting
 a Publications Request RE 350 (/files/pdf/forms/re350.pdf)
.

Additionally, most bookstores and libraries contain a real estate shelf of publications which can be helpful in preparing for
 examination.

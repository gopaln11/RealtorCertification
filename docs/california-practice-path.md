# California salesperson path for practice in Irvine

The working copy, with one folder per course and the official files saved beside each course, is [RealtorCertification](../README.md).

A working checklist to get a California real estate salesperson license and start practice based in Irvine, including Great Park. Written from official pages reviewed on October 2, 2026.

This is a study and logistics plan, not legal advice and not a DRE determination. Fees, forms, processing times, and association dues change. Before you pay a school or file an application, confirm the current page with the California Department of Real Estate (DRE). Public line: (877) 373-4542. Licensing questions: [dre.ca.gov/AskDRELicensing](https://dre.ca.gov/AskDRELicensing).

You do not need a broker license to start. You need a salesperson license, then a responsible broker who certifies your affiliation. Until that affiliation is on record, you may hold a license and still be barred from licensed work.

## What “allowed to practice” means

Two different bars. Meet the first one or the work is illegal. The second one is how resale brokerages in Irvine actually operate.

### Required by law to obtain the salesperson license and to practice

Sources: [Requirements for a salesperson license](https://www.dre.ca.gov/Examinees/RequirementsSales.html), [exam and license process](https://www.dre.ca.gov/examinees/salesperson_exam_license.html), [fees](https://www.dre.ca.gov/licensees/fees.html), [fingerprints](https://www.dre.ca.gov/licensees/fingerprint.html).

- Age 18 or older when the license is issued.
- Honest application. A criminal conviction can lead to denial. Answer the application completely. Social Security number is required so fingerprint results match the file.
- Three college-level courses, finished before you can be scheduled for the exam:
  1. Real Estate Principles
  2. Real Estate Practice
  3. One elective from the DRE list (recommendation below)
- Each course is 3 semester units or 4 quarter units, or a DRE-approved statutory course of at least 45 hours, from a regionally accredited college or a private school approved by the Real Estate Commissioner. Continuing education does not count.
- From January 1, 2024, any Real Estate Practice course used for exam qualification must include implicit bias and fair housing. The fair housing portion must include an interactive role-play in which you act as both the consumer and the real estate professional. Confirm the course you buy says it meets that requirement.
- Pass the salesperson exam.
- Submit fingerprints (Live Scan) and clear the Department of Justice and FBI review. DRE will not issue the original license until those reports are in and screened.
- Pay the exam and license fees.
- Be affiliated with a responsible California broker, and have that broker certify the affiliation, before you perform any act that requires a license. DRE will issue a license to someone who does not yet have a broker. The status is licensed with no broker affiliation. You still may not practice.

There is no separate Orange County license, Irvine license, or Great Park license.

### Required in practice by typical Irvine resale brokerages

These are not conditions of the DRE license. They are how listing and selling existing homes in Irvine actually works. Confirm each item in the brokerage agreement before you join.

- Sponsorship. The broker (or the designated officer of a corporate broker) certifies you in DRE eLicensing, or you file [Salesperson Change Application RE 214](https://dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/forms/re214.pdf). Tutorial: [Add Responsible Broker](https://secure.dre.ca.gov/tutorial/elic_tutorial_add_employ_broker.html). Only that broker may pay you for licensed work.
- Errors and omissions insurance. California does not require E&O as a condition of issuing a salesperson license. Resale brokers carry a company policy and require agents to be covered. Get the premium, the deductible, and who pays, in writing. Orange County REALTORS lists E&O options (RealCare and the NAR/Victor program) as member resources, not as a membership prerequisite: [Risk management](https://www.ocrealtors.org/risk-management).
- REALTOR association. For Irvine and the rest of Orange County, the association agents join is [Orange County REALTORS](https://www.ocrealtors.org/Membership/REALTOR). Membership includes the California Association of REALTORS (C.A.R.) and the National Association of REALTORS (NAR). C.A.R. forms are what resale contracts in this market are written on. Orange County REALTORS also offers an MLS-only salesperson category at a lower price. Many resale brokers still require full REALTOR membership. Ask the broker which category they accept.
- MLS. The multiple listing service used through Orange County REALTORS is CRMLS (California Regional Multiple Listing Service). New members complete association orientation, NAR Code of Ethics training, and a fair-housing course within 90 days: [New REALTOR education](https://www.ocrealtors.org/education/new-realtor-requirements). CRMLS access also expects an orientation of no more than eight classroom hours within 90 days of access.
- Lockbox access. Orange County REALTORS uses Supra eKEY for lockboxes in most of Southern California except San Diego County. The membership page lists a professional monthly fee (the October 2026 page shows both $16.61 and $16.98 depending on the block), plus a $50 initial activation fee, tax, and daily proration. Read the figure on the day you join.
- City of Irvine business license, if you operate as a business in the city. This is a city tax license, not a real estate license. The City’s business licensing page says a home business needs a business license and must meet home-occupation standards, and it lists a small-team fee (fewer than 10 employees) of $78.37, which includes the $4 state ADA fee, for a 12-month term: [Business licensing](https://www.cityofirvine.gov/community-development/business-licensing). Home-occupation rules are in Irvine Zoning Code Chapter 2-14. Whether a 1099 agent must hold their own city license, while a W-2 employee is covered by the broker, is a question for the City (949-724-7128, businesslicense@cityofirvine.org) after you know how the broker will pay you. Also check the CC&Rs for your home. Do not treat another city’s real-estate-agent tax FAQ as an Irvine rule.

### Not required now

- A broker license. Later section.
- A mortgage loan originator (MLO) endorsement. Needed only if you will solicit or negotiate residential mortgage loans. Selling homes does not require it.
- NAR designations (CRS, ABR, SRS, and the rest).
- A college degree in real estate. Your engineering degree and MBA do not by themselves waive the three salesperson courses. A prior college course can sometimes count as the elective. See below.

## Recommended sequence and time

Plan on about four to six months from the day you start the first course until you can legally practice resale, if you study at a steady part-time pace and pass on the first attempt. A tighter three-month version is realistic only if the school lets you move as soon as the hours are done, La Palma has a seat, and fingerprints clear without a delay. The legal outer bound is longer: once DRE receives a paid application, you have two years to finish any missing qualifications and pass the exam. That window is a safety net, not the plan.

The estimates below assume you can give this about 15 hours a week around family. The material is new vocabulary and California procedure, not a remedial academic course. Your advantage is reading speed, finance, and having already run a team. Use it on the practice exams, not on extra certificates.

| Step | What you do | Time | Money |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Read the DRE requirements page. Decide the elective. Check old transcripts. | 2–3 days | $0 |
| 1 | Real Estate Principles | 2–3 weeks | Course fee |
| 2 | Real Estate Finance, unless DRE accepts a transcript course | 2–3 weeks | Course fee, or $0 |
| 3 | Real Estate Practice, including the live fair-housing role-play | 2–3 weeks | Course fee |
| 4 | Timed exam drills on the seven DRE topics | 2–3 weeks, overlap the end of Practice | Prep subscription if you buy one |
| 5 | File the combined exam/license application. Live Scan. | Same week courses are done | $450 to DRE + fingerprint charges |
| 6 | Wait for DRE to qualify you, then sit the exam at La Palma | Several weeks of DRE time, then one morning | $0 if you pass; $100 to retake |
| 7 | Broker certifies affiliation. Join Orange County REALTORS / CRMLS. Confirm E&O and the city license. | 1–3 weeks after the license number exists | Association dues, eKEY, E&O as the broker charges, city fee if required |

DRE processing time is posted weekly and is often longer than the department would like. Fingerprint review is outside DRE’s control. Read [current processing timeframes](https://www.dre.ca.gov/proc_time.htm) the week you apply. Do not file twice. A duplicate online and mail application can charge you twice, and fees are not refundable.

## Step 0 — Choose the third course before you enroll

Required courses, in this order:

1. **Real Estate Principles.** Foundation: ownership, encumbrances, agency, contracts, fair housing vocabulary. Do this first.
2. **Real Estate Finance.** Recommended elective. Your MBA already covers time value, ratios, and capital structure. This course is the California layer those classes did not teach: deeds of trust (the usual California security instrument, rather than a mortgage), loan types, government programs, TILA/RESPA and other credit laws, points, and loan brokerage. Financing is about 9% of the salesperson exam, and it feeds the 14% valuation and financial-analysis section. Take the statutory Real Estate Finance course. Do not assume a general MBA finance course is the same class. It is not on the DRE elective list under that name.
3. **Real Estate Practice, last.** Largest exam section (about 25%), plus the required implicit-bias and interactive fair-housing component. Agency, disclosures, trust funds, advertising, and license procedure should be fresh on exam day.

### Acceptable alternatives to Finance

Take one of these only if it fits a reason below. Still study financing on your own, because the exam tests it either way.

- **Legal Aspects of Real Estate.** Best alternative if you want the shortest later path to a broker license. It is a required broker course, so it would not be wasted. Heavier reading. A good fit if you would rather learn the rules than the loan products first.
- **An existing Accounting, Business Law, or Economics course** already on an official college transcript, at 3 semester units or 4 quarter units. DRE will evaluate equivalency if you submit the transcript and an official catalog description. Do this check before you pay for a third course. A foreign degree has to go through a DRE-approved credentials evaluation ([RE 223](https://www.dre.ca.gov/Examinees/RequirementsSales.html)).
- **Property Management, Escrows, or Common Interest Developments.** Irvine and Great Park are full of HOAs, escrow closings, and rental property. Those topics are real, and they are narrower on the salesperson exam than finance or legal aspects. Better as later broker electives than as the only third course now.
- Other electives DRE accepts, if you already have a reason: Real Estate Appraisal, Real Estate Economics, Real Estate Office Administration, General Accounting, Business Law, Mortgage Loan Brokering and Lending, Computer Applications in Real Estate. Full list: [salesperson requirements](https://www.dre.ca.gov/Examinees/RequirementsSales.html) and [RE 423 (Rev. 1/26)](https://dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/forms/re423.pdf).

Before you pay any private school, look the sponsor up in the [DRE statutory course search](https://secure.dre.ca.gov/publicasp/cestatutory.asp). The course must appear there, or it must be a 3-semester-unit or 4-quarter-unit class at a regionally accredited college. Ignore a school page that says the California exam is run by PSI, or that quotes an exam fee other than the DRE fee page. Some marketing pages are simply wrong.

College credit is optional for you. You already have the degrees. An approved online package is the faster route. A California community college is often cheaper per unit if a section is actually open; confirm the current course, the unit value, and the calendar with the college. DRE’s note on college options is on [Education course availability](https://www.dre.ca.gov/Examinees/EducationCourses.html). Private-school statutory courses usually do not transfer as college credit.

## What to study, and with which materials

Use official outlines for scope. Use a question bank for reps. Do not try to memorize the entire Real Estate Law book.

### Official, and free

- **Exam candidate handbook.** [Instructions to License Applicants (RE 4)](https://dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/re4.pdf). The PDF says revised June 2020 and warns that legislation can make the booklet stale. Where it disagrees with a current DRE web page, follow the web page. The shorter current checklist is [Obtaining a Salesperson License (RE 4A, Rev. 6/24)](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/Obtaining_Salesperson_License.pdf) and the [exam/license instructions (RE 435A, Rev. 6/24)](https://dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/forms/re435a.pdf).
- **Exam blueprint.** [Salesperson examination content](https://dre.ca.gov/Examinees/SalesExamContent.html) and [Examination Description RE 425 (Rev. 6/25)](https://dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/forms/re425.pdf). Weights for the salesperson exam:

  | Area | Approx. share |
  | --- | --- |
  | Practice of real estate and disclosures, including specialty areas | 25% |
  | Laws of agency and fiduciary duties | 17% |
  | Property ownership and land use controls | 15% |
  | Property valuation and financial analysis | 14% |
  | Contracts | 12% |
  | Financing | 9% |
  | Transfer of property | 8% |

  Put study hours in that order. Agency, practice, and contracts are more than half the exam.
- **Current law, as a reference, not a textbook.** [2026 Real Estate Law](https://www.dre.ca.gov/publications/RealEstateLaw.html) (statutes and Commissioner regulations in effect January 1, 2026). [DRE’s preparation page](https://www.dre.ca.gov/Examinees/Preparing.html) points examinees here. Look up a rule when a practice question turns on it. Do not read the book cover to cover before the courses.
- **Reference Book — A Real Estate Guide.** DRE last updated this book in 2010. Chapter 2, still posted by DRE, says the exam format has changed since then and points readers to the current examination-content pages: [Reference Book, chapter 2](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref02.pdf). Skip it for “what is the law now.” A course written to the current exam outline is more useful.
- **Exam-day rules.** [Taking the Exam](https://www.dre.ca.gov/Examinees/TakingExam.html).

### Exam-prep practice

After the courses, train on full exams.

- The question bank that comes with the package you bought (CE Shop Exam Prep Edge, OnlineEd’s prep course, or Kaplan’s).
- [PrepAgent](https://www.prepagent.com/) for California-specific drills, short videos, and timed exams. Check the current subscription price on their site. A third-party comparison has quoted about $79 for a month or $99 for three months; that is not a price from PrepAgent’s own page, so confirm it.
- Build one sheet of California traps and review it daily in the last two weeks: deed of trust versus mortgage; when the agency disclosure is required; Transfer Disclosure Statement and natural hazard disclosures; who may hold trust funds; who may pay a salesperson; what an unlicensed assistant may not do; advertising rules; community property and vesting; proration, commission, area, interest, and loan amount by hand.

Pass mark on practice sets: be consistently at or above 80% on timed 150-question exams before you sit. The real passing line is 70%, and a pass does not come with a score, so you want margin. The arithmetic is real-estate arithmetic. Your engineering math is more than enough. The risk is rusty hand calculation if the testing center does not allow a calculator. See the exam-day note.

### How to use the career break

Five years out of an office does not change the course list. It does change the first 90 days after you are licensed: you will want a broker who actually trains, not only a high split. Interview on that point. Your HR and startup experience is relevant to choosing a brokerage (supervision, contracts, how assistants are used) and to fair-housing compliance. It is not a substitute for the statutory courses.

## Step 5 — Application, fingerprints, fees

File only after all three courses are complete and you have copies of official transcripts or completion certificates. Unofficial transcripts are not accepted.

Prefer the combined path. One application covers the exam and the original license.

1. Create an eLicensing profile from [dre.ca.gov](https://www.dre.ca.gov/). Choose the salesperson exam/license application.
2. Upload transcripts, answer every question, pay by Visa, MasterCard, or Discover.
3. Mail is the backup: combined application RE 435, transcripts, and fee, to Department of Real Estate, Examination Section, 651 Bannon Street, Suite 503, Sacramento, CA 95811. Do not send cash. Do not also file online.

### Official fees

From the [DRE fee page](https://www.dre.ca.gov/licensees/fees.html) and [RE 206 (Rev. 7/26)](https://dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/forms/re206.pdf). Fees are not refundable (Business and Professions Code section 10207).

| Item | Amount | Who is paid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salesperson examination | $100 | DRE |
| Original salesperson license | $350 | DRE |
| Combined exam and license (RE 435) | $450 due to DRE | DRE |
| Re-examination | $100 | DRE |
| Rescheduled exam | $40 | DRE |
| Fingerprint processing | $49 | Live Scan provider, if you live in California. Not sent to DRE. |
| Live Scan operator’s rolling fee | Set by the provider | The provider. Call the site for the total. |
| On-time renewal, every four years | $350 | DRE |
| Late renewal, within two years after expiration | $525 | DRE |

A California resident’s cash to DRE for the combined filing is $450. The $49 fingerprint processing fee is paid at Live Scan on top of whatever the operator charges to take the prints. Older copies of the Commissioner’s regulations still show a lower license fee. Use the fee page, not those older regulation figures.

### Fingerprints

- Form: [Live Scan Service Request RE 237 (Rev. 8/26)](https://dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/forms/re237.pdf).
- You may be printed after you apply, or after you pass. The processing fee is not refunded if you never pass. Practical choice: print after the application is in and you have an exam date, so the DOJ report can be waiting when you pass, without printing before you are committed.
- Take RE 237 to a Live Scan site. Locations: [oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/locations](https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/locations).
- Send DRE the copy of RE 237 with Part 3 completed.
- The license is not issued until DOJ and FBI reports are received and screened.

### After you file

- DRE emails you when you are qualified. Schedule in eLicensing. Seats can be taken as late as 6:00 a.m. on exam day if a seat is open. Check dates: [exams currently scheduling](https://secure.dre.ca.gov/publicasp/currentexams.asp).
- If something is missing, you have two years from the date DRE received the application and fee to fix it and pass. After that, you file and pay again.
- [Do’s and don’ts](https://www.dre.ca.gov/Examinees/DosDonts.html): one application only; update your address in eLicensing; print the schedule notice and bring it.

## Exam day

Closest electronic center to Irvine: DRE Electronic Exam Center, 1 Centerpointe Drive, Suite 370, 3rd Floor, La Palma, CA 90623. [La Palma exam center](https://www.dre.ca.gov/MapLaPalmaEEC.html). Other electronic sites are Fresno, Oakland, Sacramento, and San Diego. Walk-ins are not accepted.

- Salesperson exam: 150 multiple-choice questions, 3 hours, four answer choices. Pass at 70% (105 correct). If you pass, you are not told the score. If you fail, you get the score and the percentage by subject area. The overall score is not the average of those percentages.
- Results on the electronic exam are given when you finish.
- Arrive about 30 minutes before the start. The La Palma page also says not to arrive more than half an hour early. Follow the schedule notice if the two instructions differ.
- Identification, current and with the legal name on the application: California driver license or DMV identification card, a U.S. or foreign passport, or a U.S. military ID. Nothing else is accepted.
- Parking for La Palma is at 8 Centerpointe Drive, top deck of the structure, and the parking permit printed on the schedule notice must be displayed.
- The [Taking the Exam](https://www.dre.ca.gov/Examinees/TakingExam.html) page lists calculators among items that are not allowed in the room (along with phones, watches, notes, and bags). Commissioner’s Regulation 2763, quoted on that same page, still describes a silent, non-programmable, pocket calculator without a printer or alphabetic keyboard as something an examinee may use. Those two instructions conflict. Practice every calculation by hand. On exam day, follow the schedule notice and the proctor. Do not argue a regulation at the door.
- Cell phones must be off and in a locker, including during any break. Copying questions is prohibited.
- If you fail, wait for the result notice, then reapply in eLicensing or with RE 415A. Fee $100. There is no cap on attempts inside the two-year life of the original application. Do not pay for a new date before the result is released.

If the combined application was clean, DRE’s salesperson instructions say the license number is issued at the exam site after a passing score, assuming nothing is still outstanding (fingerprints are the usual outstanding item). Print the certificate from eLicensing. DRE no longer issues a pocket card.

## Step 7 — From a license number to working in Irvine

You can start broker conversations while you are in the courses. You cannot do licensed work until the affiliation is certified.

### Broker

Pick a responsible broker and have them certify you: eLicensing, Add Responsible Broker, then the broker (or the corporate designated officer) certifies. If they do not certify within seven days, DRE writes to them. Until the record shows a responsible broker, status stays licensed with no broker affiliation and you cannot practice.

What to ask each Irvine brokerage, in writing:

- Who is the broker of record, and how quickly do they certify new agents?
- Training for someone returning to work after several years, not a script for a 22-year-old.
- Commission split, desk fee, transaction fee, and technology fees.
- E&O: limits, deductible, and whether you pay a share.
- Do they require full Orange County REALTORS membership, or will they allow MLS-only?
- What an unlicensed assistant in their office is allowed to do.
- Whether they sell resale, new-home product, or both.

Compensation for licensed acts comes from the broker, not from the client and not from another agent.

### Association, MLS, and lockbox

After you have the license and the broker:

1. Apply to [Orange County REALTORS as a REALTOR salesperson](https://www.ocrealtors.org/Membership/REALTOR). They need the application, an e-signed acknowledgement from you and the broker, a copy of the DRE license, a copy of your driver license, and payment.
2. Read the fee calculator on that page the day you join. Dues are prorated by start month. On the October 2, 2026 view of the page, the January–December 2026 new-member line totaled $1,656.50 (C.A.R., a C.A.R. processing fee, NAR, local dues, a $25 application fee, a $20 REALTOR Action Fund line, an MLS/OCR processing fee, CRMLS access, and a $35 CRMLS security fee). Another block on the same page totaled $1,773. Later months were lower. The $20 REALTOR Action Fund line is described by the association as voluntary. C.A.R. dues include a REALTOR Action Assessment that you may redirect in writing to a non-candidate issues fund. CRMLS access and security fees may be waived if you are already a CRMLS member.
3. Complete, within 90 days, New REALTOR orientation (Orange County REALTORS enrolls you), NAR’s free Code of Ethics course for new members, and one NAR fair-housing course (Fairhaven, At Home with Diversity, or Bias Override). Details: [new member education](https://www.ocrealtors.org/education/new-realtor-requirements).
4. Subscribe to Supra eKEY if you will open lockboxes.
5. Calendar the next year’s association renewal. For 2026, Orange County REALTORS set a January 7, 2026 due date and a $35 late fee: [dues renewal](https://www.ocrealtors.org/membership/dues-renewal). Use the current year’s page when it is posted.

Offices: Fountain Valley, 10540 Talbert Avenue, Suite 225, 714-375-9313. Laguna Hills, 949-586-6800 (confirm the street address on the association site; it has moved).

### City license

If the broker pays you as an independent contractor and you work from an Irvine home, call the City business-license line and ask whether you need your own license and a home-occupation permit. Have the broker’s classification in hand. The published small-team fee on the City page is $78.37 including the $4 state ADA fee. Apply within 60 days of starting to avoid the late fee the City lists.

## The four-year renewal cycle

The license term is four years. [How long a license lasts](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/faqs/FAQ_RealEstateLicenses.pdf). [How to renew](https://dre.ca.gov/licensees/RenewLicense.html).

First salesperson renewal, 45 clock hours of DRE-approved continuing education, completed inside the four years before renewal. From [CE requirements](https://www.dre.ca.gov/licensees/CERequirements.html):

- Four separate 3-hour courses: ethics, agency, trust fund handling, and risk management.
- One 3-hour fair-housing course with the interactive role-play as both consumer and professional.
- One 2-hour implicit-bias course.
- At least 18 hours in consumer protection.
- The remaining hours in consumer service or consumer protection, to reach 45.

Statutory pre-license courses do not renew the license. CE does not qualify you for the original exam. Keep them separate.

Second and later renewals: 45 hours again. Either one 9-hour survey covering the seven mandatory subjects (ethics, agency, trust fund handling, risk management, management and supervision, fair housing, and implicit bias) or the individual courses, plus at least 18 consumer-protection hours, and the rest consumer service or consumer protection.

Renew in eLicensing as early as 90 days before expiration. On time means the eLicensing transaction is finished, or the mailed application is postmarked, before midnight on the expiration date. On-time fee: $350. File [RE 209](https://dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/forms/re209.pdf) and [CE verification RE 251 (Rev. 2/26)](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/forms/re251.pdf) if you renew by mail. If you file a complete on-time renewal, Business and Professions Code section 10156.2 lets you keep working under the existing license until DRE says otherwise.

If you miss the date, you may renew late for two years. Late fee: $525. You may not do licensed work while the license is expired. After two years the renewal right is gone and you start over.

CE must be entered before an online renewal will go through. There is a narrow mailed extension (RE 213) for people who qualify under Business and Professions Code section 10171.2. The age-70, 30-year exemption will not apply to a first license.

## Broker license, later

Not a prerequisite. Revisit after you have been actively selling and you want to run an office or stop depending on someone else’s brokerage.

Official requirements: [broker license](https://dre.ca.gov/examinees/requirementsbroker.html) and [experience](https://dre.ca.gov/examinees/BrokerExperience.html).

- Eight college-level courses: Real Estate Practice, Legal Aspects, Real Estate Finance, Real Estate Appraisal, Real Estate Economics or Accounting, plus three from the elective group (Real Estate Principles counts). If you complete both Economics and Accounting, only two electives are required.
- If you follow this plan (Principles, Practice, Finance), three of the eight are already done. Legal Aspects, Appraisal, and Economics or Accounting remain, plus two electives (or one elective if you take both Economics and Accounting).
- Experience: two years of full-time licensed salesperson work within the five years before the broker application, documented by the responsible broker on RE 226. Holding the license for two years is not enough. Part-time work can be prorated to the two-year full-time equivalent. A four-year degree waives the experience only if the major or minor was real estate. An engineering degree or an MBA does not waive it.
- Exam: 200 questions, 4 hours, 75% to pass. Exam fee $150, original broker license $450, combined due to DRE $600, plus fingerprints only if DRE still needs a new set. On-time broker renewal is $450.

Do not take the extra five courses before you are licensed and working, unless a specific course is your salesperson elective.

## Great Park and new homes

No separate credential. Great Park Neighborhoods is resale housing and new-home sales sitting inside ordinary California license law.

- **Resale in Great Park, Woodbridge, or anywhere else in Irvine** uses the same license, the same responsible-broker rule, and, in ordinary practice, Orange County REALTORS and CRMLS. HOA documents and CC&Rs come up constantly. They are a standard disclosure and contract issue, not a local license.
- **Builder sales offices** sell that builder’s new homes. Negotiating those sales for compensation still requires a California real estate license and a responsible broker, unless a narrow statutory exemption actually applies. The exemption people sometimes misread is Business and Professions Code section 10133: a regular corporate officer or general partner, dealing with property the entity itself owns or is buying, and not for special compensation. That is not a general exemption for sales staff. Confirm how the particular builder structures the job. Some on-site roles are W-2 sales for a project brokerage. MLS membership is a brokerage requirement for resale inventory; a new-home desk that never lists on CRMLS may not make you join. Get that from the employer. There is no published City of Irvine or Great Park rule that creates a different license for builder sales.

## Designations worth later, not now

Skip these until you have a license, a broker, and some closed transactions. They do not qualify you for the exam and they are not required to practice.

- **ABR** (Accredited Buyer’s Representative) and **SRS** (Seller Representative Specialist), when you know which side of the table you are building a business on.
- **CRS** (Council of Residential Specialists), after you have the production it requires. This is a later professional credential, not a starting class.
- **CNE** (Certified New Home Sales Professional, through the home-builders’ education arm), only if you take a builder-sales job and the builder values it.
- **SRES**, if a real share of your work is with older sellers in Irvine villages.
- Fair-housing designations such as At Home with Diversity overlap training you already have to complete for the Practice course, for CE, and for new-member orientation. Do not buy them twice in year one.

## Checklist you can tick

- [ ] Read [salesperson requirements](https://www.dre.ca.gov/Examinees/RequirementsSales.html) and the [fee page](https://www.dre.ca.gov/licensees/fees.html).
- [ ] If an old transcript might cover Accounting, Business Law, or Economics, gather the official transcript and catalog description before buying a third course.
- [ ] Enroll in Principles, then Finance (or the accepted alternative), then Practice. Confirm the school on the [statutory course search](https://secure.dre.ca.gov/publicasp/cestatutory.asp) and confirm Practice includes implicit bias and the interactive fair-housing role-play.
- [ ] Finish the courses. Save completion certificates and transcript copies.
- [ ] Drill timed 150-question exams until you are steady at 80% or better. Use [the content outline](https://dre.ca.gov/Examinees/SalesExamContent.html) and [RE 425](https://dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/forms/re425.pdf).
- [ ] File one combined application in eLicensing. Pay $450. Do not also mail a second application.
- [ ] Live Scan with [RE 237](https://dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/forms/re237.pdf). Pay the $49 processing fee plus the operator’s fee at the site.
- [ ] Schedule La Palma. Print the schedule notice. Bring current photo ID in your legal name.
- [ ] Pass. Print the license certificate from eLicensing.
- [ ] Broker certifies affiliation in eLicensing. Read the contractor agreement: split, fees, E&O.
- [ ] Join Orange County REALTORS, get CRMLS access, finish orientation, ethics, and fair housing within 90 days, and turn on eKEY if you will show resale homes.
- [ ] Ask the City of Irvine whether you need a business license and home-occupation permit for the way this broker pays you.
- [ ] Put the four-year expiration date on a calendar. Plan the 45 hours of CE before that date. Renew on time for $350.

# 7. Renewal and continuing education

The salesperson license term is four years. Put the expiration date on a calendar when the license is issued. This unit is legally required to keep practicing. It is not part of qualifying for the original exam.

First salesperson renewal: 45 clock hours of DRE-approved continuing education, completed inside the four years before renewal. From the saved CE page:

- Four separate 3-hour courses: ethics, agency, trust fund handling, and risk management.
- One 3-hour fair-housing course with the interactive role-play as both consumer and professional.
- One 2-hour implicit-bias course.
- At least 18 hours in consumer protection.
- The remaining hours in consumer service or consumer protection, to reach 45.

Statutory pre-license courses do not renew the license. CE does not qualify you for the original exam.

Second and later renewals: 45 hours again. Either one 9-hour survey covering the seven mandatory subjects (ethics, agency, trust fund handling, risk management, management and supervision, fair housing, and implicit bias) or the individual courses, plus at least 18 consumer-protection hours, and the rest consumer service or consumer protection. The saved page states this for license expiration dates on or after January 1, 2023, and for late renewals after that date.

Renew in eLicensing as early as 90 days before expiration. On time means the eLicensing transaction is finished, or the mailed application is postmarked, before midnight on the expiration date. On-time salesperson renewal fee: $350. Late renewal within two years after expiration: $525. You may not do licensed work while the license is expired. After two years the renewal right is gone and you start over. If you file a complete on-time renewal, Business and Professions Code section 10156.2 lets you keep working under the existing license until DRE says otherwise.

Mail uses RE 209 and CE verification RE 251. There is a narrow mailed extension, RE 213, for people who qualify under Business and Professions Code section 10171.2. It cannot be completed online. The age-70, 30-year exemption will not apply to a first license.

Broker first-renewal CE adds a 3-hour management and supervision course. That is a later problem. It is described on the same CE page.

## What still has to be purchased

The 45 hours, from DRE-approved CE providers, during the four-year term. Nothing to buy now. No CE package was downloaded.

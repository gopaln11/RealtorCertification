# RealtorCertification

A California salesperson path, then a later broker path, with the free official materials that can actually be downloaded. The goal is a license and a responsible-broker affiliation so you can practice resale in Irvine, including Great Park. There is no separate Irvine license and no separate Great Park license.

This folder is a study and logistics plan. It is not legal advice and not a Department of Real Estate (DRE) determination. Fees, forms, processing times, and association dues change. Before you pay a school or file an application, confirm the live page. DRE public line: (877) 373-4542. Licensing questions: [dre.ca.gov/AskDRELicensing](https://dre.ca.gov/AskDRELicensing).

The longer working checklist, written from the same October 2, 2026 review, is [California practice path](docs/california-practice-path.md). This README is the course map. That checklist is the day-to-day sequence, fee notes, and Irvine brokerage questions. Neither file replaces the DRE page.

## What “allowed to practice” means

Two different bars.

**Legally required to obtain the salesperson license and to do licensed work.** Age 18 when the license is issued. An honest application. Three college-level courses finished before DRE will schedule the exam: Real Estate Principles, Real Estate Practice, and one elective. From January 1, 2024, any Real Estate Practice course used for exam qualification must include implicit bias and fair housing, and the fair housing portion must include an interactive role-play as both the consumer and the real estate professional. Pass the salesperson exam. Live Scan fingerprints, with Department of Justice and FBI reports screened by DRE. Pay the exam and license fees. Be affiliated with a responsible California broker, and have that broker certify the affiliation, before any act that requires a license. DRE will issue a license with no broker affiliation. That status still does not allow practice.

**Required in practice by a typical Irvine resale brokerage, and not a condition of the DRE license.** Broker sponsorship on the DRE record. Errors and omissions coverage on the broker’s terms. Orange County REALTORS membership (or whichever membership category that broker accepts), California Association of REALTORS and National Association of REALTORS through that membership, and CRMLS access. Lockbox access if you will open Supra eKEY boxes. A City of Irvine business license if the way the broker pays you means you are operating a business in the city. Confirm each item in the brokerage agreement and, for the city license, with the City.

**Later.** A broker license. A mortgage loan originator endorsement, only if you will solicit or negotiate residential mortgage loans. NAR designations.

An engineering degree and an MBA do not waive the three salesperson courses. A prior college course in accounting, business law, or economics can sometimes count as the elective if DRE accepts the official transcript and catalog description. A general MBA finance course is not on the elective list under that name. Do not assume it substitutes for Real Estate Finance.

## Time

Plan on about four to six months from the first course until you can legally practice resale, at about 15 hours a week, if you pass on the first attempt. A tighter three-month version depends on the school letting you move as soon as the hours are done, a seat at the La Palma exam center, and fingerprints clearing without a delay. Once DRE receives a paid application, you have two years to finish missing qualifications and pass. That window is a safety net, not the plan.

The material is new vocabulary and California procedure. Use the finance and operations background on the practice exams and on choosing a brokerage. It does not shorten the statutory course list.

## How the free materials fit

Each numbered folder has a short README and a `materials/` directory. The READMEs say what was saved and what still has to be purchased.

Statutory courses are sold by a regionally accredited college or by a private school on the [DRE statutory course search](https://secure.dre.ca.gov/publicasp/cestatutory.asp). An approved statutory course is at least 45 hours. A college class must be 3 semester units or 4 quarter units. Continuing education does not qualify you for the original exam. Pre-license courses do not renew the license. This library does not contain Kaplan, The CE Shop, PrepAgent, OnlineEd, Allied, or any other paid course. Those packages were not downloaded.

The DRE Reference Book was last updated in 2010 (copyright 2010, California Department of Real Estate). Chapter 2, filed in exam prep, says the examination format has changed since publication and points readers to the current examination-content pages. Chapters are filed under the course they support. Use them for vocabulary and structure. Do not use them as a statement of current law. There is no current DRE web index for the book. `https://www.dre.ca.gov/publications/ReferenceBook.html` returned HTTP 404 on October 2, 2026. Chapters `ref00.pdf` through `ref27.pdf` downloaded. `ref28.pdf` and above returned HTTP 404.

## Irvine and Great Park

Resale in Great Park, Woodbridge, or anywhere else in Irvine uses the same salesperson license, the same responsible-broker rule, and, in ordinary practice, Orange County REALTORS and CRMLS. HOA documents and CC&Rs are a disclosure and contract issue, not a local license.

Builder sales offices still sit inside California license law. Negotiating those sales for compensation requires a California real estate license and a responsible broker unless a narrow statutory exemption actually applies. Business and Professions Code section 10133 is not a general exemption for sales staff. Confirm how that builder structures the job. A new-home desk that never lists on CRMLS may not require MLS membership. Get that from the employer.

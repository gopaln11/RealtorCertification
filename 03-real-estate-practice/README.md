# 3. Real Estate Practice

Third statutory course. Take it last so agency, disclosures, trust funds, advertising, and license procedure are fresh on exam day.

Practice is legally required. It is the largest salesperson exam section, about 25 percent. From January 1, 2024, a Real Estate Practice course used to qualify for the exam must include implicit bias and fair housing, and the fair housing portion must include an interactive role-play in which you act as both the consumer and the real estate professional. Confirm the course you buy says it meets that requirement. DRE’s February 16, 2023 advisory on Senate Bill 1495 is saved here.

Plan on two to three weeks at about 15 hours a week. HR and startup experience is relevant to fair-housing compliance and to how a brokerage supervises assistants. It is not a substitute for the course.

This is not legal advice. Requirements page: [requirements-salesperson.md](../05-license-fingerprints-broker-sponsorship/materials/requirements-salesperson.md). Roadmap: [README](../README.md). Working checklist: [california-practice-path.md](../docs/california-practice-path.md).

## What still has to be purchased

The Real Estate Practice course, from a regionally accredited college or from a school on the [statutory course search](https://secure.dre.ca.gov/publicasp/cestatutory.asp), at least 45 hours or 3 semester / 4 quarter units. The interactive role-play is part of that course. It is not in these PDFs. No commercial Practice course was downloaded.

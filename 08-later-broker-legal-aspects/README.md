# 8. Later: Legal Aspects of Real Estate

Not a prerequisite for selling homes. Come back to this after you are licensed and working, unless you choose Legal Aspects as the salesperson elective instead of Finance.

For a broker license, Legal Aspects is one of the eight required college-level courses. The official list is [broker-requirements.md](../12-later-broker-license-and-exam/materials/broker-requirements.md). If this path is followed (Principles, Practice, Finance), Legal Aspects is still ahead, along with Appraisal, Economics or Accounting, and the remaining electives.

DRE does not publish a free Legal Aspects course. The statutory course still has to be purchased from a regionally accredited college or a school on the [statutory course search](https://secure.dre.ca.gov/publicasp/cestatutory.asp), 3 semester units, 4 quarter units, or at least 45 hours. No paid Legal Aspects course was downloaded.

The current legal text is the 2026 law set: [shared-2026-real-estate-law](../shared-2026-real-estate-law/). Contracts, title, agency, and disclosures are already filed under Principles and Practice. This folder keeps the landlord-and-tenant chapter, which is the piece that is not already sitting in those courses.

Reference Book chapter 9 is copyright 2010 and outdated for current law.

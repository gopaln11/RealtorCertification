# 11. Later: remaining electives

Broker applicants need three electives from the statutory group, or two if both Real Estate Economics and Accounting are completed. Real Estate Principles counts, so one slot is already filled if this salesperson plan is followed. The other required broker courses are Practice, Legal Aspects, Finance, Appraisal, and Economics or Accounting. Practice and Finance are in the salesperson plan. The rest are the later folders.

Do not take these as extra certificates before you are licensed and working. Irvine HOAs, escrows, and rentals are real. They are narrower on the salesperson exam than Finance or Legal Aspects, which is why they are not the recommended third course now.

DRE’s broker elective list, from the requirements page saved in the broker-license folder, is: Real Estate Principles, Business Law, Property Management, Escrow, Real Estate Office Administration, Mortgage Loan Brokering and Lending, Advanced Legal Aspects of Real Estate, Advanced Real Estate Finance, Advanced Real Estate Appraisal, Computer Applications in Real Estate, and Common Interest Developments. The salesperson elective list is similar and is on [requirements-salesperson.md](../05-license-fingerprints-broker-sponsorship/materials/requirements-salesperson.md). It uses the name General Accounting, and it does not include the “Advanced” titles. Read the page that matches the license you are applying for.

There is no free DRE outline that replaces those courses. Nothing in this folder is a purchased elective. Office administration, computer applications, business law, and the advanced courses have no separate free textbook here.

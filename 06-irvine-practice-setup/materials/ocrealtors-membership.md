Only REALTORS® and Affiliates are considered “members” of the Orange County REALTORS® and have access to exclusive member benefits.

### Ways to Join:

#### Online (Recommended)

Complete our online application (/membership/REALTOR/application); then download our Online Application Checklist (https://www.ocrealtors.org/sites/default/files/2020-10/Online%20REALTOR%20Application%20Checklist_Fillable_101620_0.pdf) for instructions on your next steps. We’ll contact you to finalize your membership. You and your broker will be sent an Application Acknowledgement to be e-Signed. To accomplish this, we’ll need your email and your Broker’s email.

#### Fax

Send us your application (https://www.ocrealtors.org/sites/default/files/2025-10/REALTOR%20Applications_fillable_rev100625.pdf) then download our Faxed Application Checklist (https://www.ocrealtors.org/sites/default/files/2020-10/Faxed%20REALTOR%20Application%20Checklist_Fillable_101520.pdf) for instructions on your next steps. We’ll contact you to finalize your membership.

### What’s Required?

- Completed Application (https://www.ocrealtors.org/sites/default/files/2025-10/REALTOR%20Applications_fillable_rev100625.pdf)
- E-Signed Application Acknowledgement (for faxed and online applications only, this will be emailed to you and your broker)
- Copy of your DRE License
- Copy of your Driver’s License
- Credit Card Payment Form (for online or faxed applications) (https://www.ocrealtors.org/sites/default/files/2021-06/2021%20Credit%20Card%20Auth.Form_.pdf)
- Letter of Good Standing (transfers from other REALTOR® Associations)

### About Dues and Fees

The total amount shown applies to new members. Payment plan options may be available for your membership type. If you are reinstating your membership, please contact us for the appropriate total. Orange County REALTORS® dues / MLS fees may be paid by AMEX, Visa, MasterCard, Discover, Check, or Cash.

To open lockboxes, you will need to subscribe to the monthly eKEY service on your smartphone. Key leases may be paid by AMEX, Visa, MasterCard, Discover. No check, cash or money orders. Lockboxes are not included and may be purchased separately.

Military Discount for New REALTOR® Members: When joining Orange County REALTORS® for the first time, the Association will offer a one-time waiver of Orange County REALTORS® local dues and Orange County REALTORS® processing fees for members on Active Military Duty and/or their spouses. Also, when joining Orange County REALTORS® for the first time, the Association will offer a one-time waiver of Orange County REALTORS® processing fees and a one-time, half-off of Orange County REALTORS® dues to U.S. Military Veterans. We'll need proof of Active Duty or Veteran status at time of joining.

California Association of REALTOR® Dues: C.A.R. membership dues total includes a REALTOR® Action Assessment ($168 of $342), a mandatory state political assessment. Members may elect in writing to have their RAA amount go toward C.A.R.'s general fund for non-candidate political issues. This fund is used for educational and mobilization efforts on issues specifically impacting REALTORS® and similar efforts that do not involve contribution to candidates.

REALTOR® Action Fund Contribution: Your voluntary contribution to the REALTOR® Action Fund is not just a donation, it is an investment in the continued success of your business. The ideas, actions, and decisions of elected officials at all levels of government directly affect the real estate industry, housing availability, and your business. Elected officials at the city, state, and national level make decisions about real estate that range from the mundane to the monumental and include open house sign size and placement, land use and zoning, business licensing requirements, point-of-sale mandates, property tax assessments, lending practices, mortgage interest rates, and the mortgage interest deduction.

To educate both politicians and the public, the California Association of REALTORS® (C.A.R.) established the REALTOR® Action Fund (RAF) to which member REALTORS® contribute. Expenditures from this fund ensure your ability to do business and protect your pocketbook. For example:

•       At the state level, C.A.R. was successful in protecting your Independent Contractor Status when the State Legislature passed Assembly Bill 5 and in defeating rent control during the past election.

•       At the national level, NAR has been active in protecting the National Flood Insurance Program and in preserving the mortgage interest deduction.

The true cost of doing business is $148.

### REALTOR® Orientation Requirement

All REALTORS® are required to attend the Association’s Orientation Program, which provides an overview of your member benefits and an introduction to the Multiple Listing Services’ Rules and Regulations. Members are instructed on the National Association of REALTORS®’ Code of Ethics and Standards of Business Practice. You will be scheduled for a session upon joining.

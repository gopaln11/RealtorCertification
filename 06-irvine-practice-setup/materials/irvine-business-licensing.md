### Business Licensing & Success in Irvine

Service Announcement: PRISM Launching October 19

The City of Irvine is committed to supporting our residents and community partners by providing transparent, efficient, and accessible online tools. On October 19, the City will launch the first phase of PRISM—the Public Resource & Integrated System for Management.

In Phase 1, local businesses can use PRISM 24/7 from any device to apply for a new business license, renew an existing license, or report a code enforcement concern. Future phases will expand digital services to include Planning and Permitting.

Important System Maintenance Notice: 

To prepare for the launch on October 19, business licensing services will undergo a planned service interruption from October 5 through October 19. Online business licensing services will be unavailable to the public during this maintenance period.

Full services will resume under PRISM on October 19. Please check back closer to launch for additional details and direct portal access.

### Phase 1: Your Essential Pre-Check

Protect Your Investment. Verify your location’s zoning before you sign a lease. The most common cause of application denial or processing delay is a "zoning mismatch." Confirm your business activity is permitted at your address before proceeding.

- Step 0: Verify Zoning — If you plan to locate your business in Irvine, contact the Planning Team at 949-724-6308 or planning@cityofirvine.org (Open in new window) to discuss zoning requirements. (mailto:planningcounter@cityofirvine.org)

- Step 1: Gather Your IDs — Have your Federal EIN and any State Professional Licenses ready in digital format (PDF or Photo).

### Phase 2: Fees & Application

Once you have confirmed your zoning and have your ID documents ready, you can complete your application online in minutes.

Licenses are issued for a 12-month term starting from the first day of your application month.

Frequently Asked Questions: Finding Your Answers Fast

- Do I need a license for a home business? Yes. You must meet the Home Occupation Standards to ensure your business is compatible with your residential neighborhood. (/sites/default/files/irvine/repository/40-27_0.pdf)

- Can I sell hemp products? Products with no more than 0.3% THC are permitted per Federal law; please have your product labels ready for review.

- Is there a fee waiver for non-profit businesses? Yes. Please provide your IRS 501(c)(3) Determination Letter with your application to waive standard fees.

- Do I need to display my business license? Yes, your license must be displayed in a conspicuous place at your business location.

- Where Can I find a list of existing Licenses? Public information related to business licenses can be found using the links below. Business License Search (Open in new window) Full List of Licensed Businesses in the City (Open in new window) (https://secure.cityofirvine.org/websearch/)

### Deadlines & Penalties

Stay ahead of the clock to keep your costs low. We will send you a courtesy renewal notice 45 days before your business license expires.

- New Businesses: Apply within 60 days of your start date to avoid late fees (starting at $111).

- Renewals: A $70.45 penalty is assessed 60 days after business license expiration and continues to accrue annually.

Contact the Licensing Team: 949-724-7128 or businesslicense@cityofirvine.org (Open in new window) (mailto:businesslicense@cityofirvine.org)

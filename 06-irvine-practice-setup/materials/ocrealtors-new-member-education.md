## New REALTOR® Education Requirements

New REALTOR® members are required to complete New REALTOR® Orientation, Code of Ethics Training, and Fair Housing Training within 90 days of joining or reinstating membership.  Members transferring from another Association without a recorded date on a letter of good standing or those who have lapsed 5 years or more, will need to complete this online orientation with Orange County REALTORS®.

- Orientation - New/ reinstating REALTORS® will be automatically registered for an online orientation upon joining. An email with instructions and credentials for the module will be sent to valid email address as entered on your application, within 24 hours of membership activation. Reminders will be sent until the module has been completed. If you need Technical Support with the online orientation program, please contact Tech Support at 1-877-762-9322 or send an email to, support@learninglibrary.com . (mailto:support@learninglibrary.com)
- Code of Ethics - Access NAR’s Online Code of Ethics training on their website . Complete "Code of Ethics Course for New Members (Free)" option. If you forgot your username or password, please reset it or contact NAR for assistance. (https://www.nar.realtor/about-nar/governing-documents/code-of-ethics/code-of-ethics-training)
- Fair Housing - Access NAR’s Online Fair Housing training on their website . Complete one of the NAR courses online - Fairhaven, At Home with Diversity (AHWD), or Bias Override. If you forgot your username or password, please reset it or contact NAR for assistance. (https://www.nar.realtor/about-nar/governing-documents/code-of-ethics/code-of-ethics-training/fair-housing-requirement)If you have questions regarding the requirements, please contact education@ocrealtors.org (mailto:education@ocrealtors.org).

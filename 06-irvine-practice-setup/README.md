# 6. Irvine practice setup

This unit starts after the license number exists and the responsible broker will certify you. You can interview brokerages during the courses. You cannot do licensed work until the affiliation is on the DRE record.

None of this is a condition of DRE issuing the salesperson license. It is how listing and selling existing homes in Irvine, including Great Park, actually works. Confirm each item in the brokerage agreement. This is not legal advice, and it is not a City of Irvine determination.

Roadmap: [README](../README.md). Working checklist, including the brokerage interview questions: [california-practice-path.md](../docs/california-practice-path.md).

## Association, MLS, E&O, city license

**Orange County REALTORS.** The saved membership page is the REALTOR salesperson or broker-associate application. It asks for the application, an e-signed acknowledgement from you and the broker, a copy of the DRE license, a copy of your driver license, and payment. On the October 2, 2026 capture, the January–December 2026 total on that page is $1,656.50, and another block on the same page totals $1,773. Later months are lower. Supra eKEY figures appear as both $16.61 and $16.98 depending on the block, plus a $50 initial activation fee, tax, and daily proration. Read the saved page and the live page the day you join. The $20 REALTOR Action Fund line is on the fee table. Treat it the way the association’s own page describes it, and read that page rather than a summary.

The working checklist notes an MLS-only salesperson category at a lower price. That category is not spelled out on the REALTOR membership page saved here. Ask the broker which category they accept, and confirm it with the association.

**New-member education.** The saved page requires New REALTOR orientation, NAR Code of Ethics training for new members, and one NAR fair-housing course (Fairhaven, At Home with Diversity, or Bias Override) within 90 days of joining or reinstating.

**CRMLS.** The MLS named on the Orange County REALTORS fee table is CRMLS. The rules and the public “how to join” page returned HTTP 403 on October 2, 2026. See [crmls-rules-not-downloaded.md](materials/crmls-rules-not-downloaded.md). Do not treat that note as the rules. The working checklist says CRMLS access also expects an orientation of no more than eight classroom hours within 90 days of access. Confirm that on the live rules when you can open them.

**E&O.** California does not require errors and omissions insurance as a condition of issuing a salesperson license. Resale brokers carry a company policy and require agents to be covered. Get the premium, the deductible, and who pays, in writing. The Orange County REALTORS risk-management page lists RealCare and the NAR/Victor program as member resources. That page is saved. Vendor sites and a 2019 association flyer were not downloaded. Membership in the association is not the same thing as an E&O policy.

**City of Irvine business license.** This is a city tax license, not a real estate license. The saved city page says a home business needs a license and must meet home-occupation standards. The small-team fee (fewer than 10 employees) on that page is $78.37, including the $4 state ADA fee, for a 12-month term. New businesses: apply within 60 days of the start date to avoid the late fee the City lists. The page also says online licensing is scheduled to be interrupted from October 5 through October 19, 2026, for a PRISM launch on October 19. Read the live page before you apply. Licensing desk: 949-724-7128, businesslicense@cityofirvine.org. Zoning check before you commit to a location: 949-724-6308, planning@cityofirvine.org.

Whether a 1099 agent must hold their own city license, while a W-2 employee is covered by the broker, is a question for the City after you know how the broker will pay you. The home-occupation application says issuance does not override CC&Rs. Read your own CC&Rs. Do not treat another city’s real-estate-agent tax FAQ as an Irvine rule.

The file saved as `home-occupation-license-application.pdf` is the PDF the city page links under the label “Home Occupation Standards.” The document title on the PDF is “Home Occupation License Application,” and the standards are the conditions the applicant checks. Original URL: https://www.cityofirvine.gov/sites/default/files/irvine/repository/40-27_0.pdf.

Broker certification itself is in [05-license-fingerprints-broker-sponsorship](../05-license-fingerprints-broker-sponsorship/), including RE 214 and the eLicensing tutorial. HOA background for Great Park resale is [re39.pdf](../03-real-estate-practice/materials/re39.pdf). New-home development, as a later elective topic, is [ref23.pdf](../11-later-broker-remaining-electives/materials/ref23.pdf). There is no separate Great Park credential.

## What still has to be purchased

Association dues, CRMLS access, eKEY, and E&O as the broker charges them. The city fee if the City says you need the license. No paid course.

# 2. Real Estate Finance

Recommended elective, taken after Principles and before Practice.

One elective is legally required. Finance is the recommendation. The salesperson exam weights financing at about 9 percent, and it feeds the 14 percent valuation and financial-analysis section. The California layer is deeds of trust, loan types, government programs, credit-law disclosures, points, and loan brokerage. An MBA already covers time value and capital structure. It does not replace this course, and “MBA finance” is not the name of a DRE elective.

Plan on two to three weeks at about 15 hours a week if you take the course. If DRE accepts a transcript course instead, this block can be zero new tuition, and you still study financing for the exam.

This is not legal advice. Roadmap: [README](../README.md). Working checklist: [california-practice-path.md](../docs/california-practice-path.md).

## Alternatives to this elective

Take one of these only for a reason. The exam tests financing either way.

- **Legal Aspects of Real Estate.** Best alternative if you want the shortest later path to a broker license. It is a required broker course. Heavier reading. Filed as a later course at [08-later-broker-legal-aspects](../08-later-broker-legal-aspects/).
- **An existing Accounting, Business Law, or Economics course** already on an official college transcript, at 3 semester units or 4 quarter units. DRE evaluates equivalency from the transcript plus an official catalog description. Do this check before you pay for a third course. Copies of official transcripts are what the requirements page says are generally acceptable. Unofficial transcripts are not.
- **Foreign education.** If the degree or the course was earned outside the United States, DRE requires a credentials evaluation. That form is [re223.pdf](materials/re223.pdf), RE 223 (Rev. 6/24). A United States engineering degree or MBA does not use this form. The form also says a four-year degree substitutes for broker *experience* only when the major or minor is real estate. That is a later question, and it does not waive salesperson courses.
- **Property Management, Escrows, or Common Interest Developments.** Real in Irvine, narrower on the salesperson exam than finance or legal aspects. Better as later broker electives. See [11-later-broker-remaining-electives](../11-later-broker-remaining-electives/).

The full salesperson elective list is on [requirements-salesperson.md](../05-license-fingerprints-broker-sponsorship/materials/requirements-salesperson.md) and on RE 423 in the license folder.

## What still has to be purchased

The statutory Real Estate Finance course, unless DRE accepts a transcript substitute. Same rule as Principles: accredited college at 3 semester or 4 quarter units, or a course on the [statutory course search](https://secure.dre.ca.gov/publicasp/cestatutory.asp) of at least 45 hours. No commercial finance course was downloaded. DRE does not publish a free finance textbook that replaces the school course.

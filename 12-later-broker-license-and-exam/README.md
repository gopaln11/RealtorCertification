# 12. Later: broker license and exam

Not required to practice. A salesperson license plus a responsible broker is what Irvine resale requires. Revisit this after you have been actively selling and you want to run an office or stop depending on someone else’s brokerage.

Official pages saved here:

- Eight college-level courses. See [08](../08-later-broker-legal-aspects/) through [11](../11-later-broker-remaining-electives/) for how the salesperson plan maps onto that list.
- Experience: a minimum of two years full-time licensed salesperson experience within the last five years, or two years of unlicensed equivalent experience, or a four-year degree with a major or minor in real estate. Holding the license for two years is not enough. The experience page is saved here. An engineering degree or an MBA does not waive the experience requirement, because the major or minor was not real estate.
- Document licensed experience on RE 226, completed by the responsible broker. RE 227 is the equivalent-experience form. It is saved so the requirements page’s reference is not a broken link. It is not the form for ordinary licensed salesperson experience, and it is not a way to count a startup or an HR career as broker experience unless DRE’s experience page actually fits those facts. Read the saved experience page before you use it.
- Exam: the broker content page says an applicant must correctly answer at least 75 percent. The working checklist, from the same DRE review, states 200 questions and 4 hours. The content-page category weights posted on October 2, 2026 match the salesperson weights (practice about 25 percent, agency about 17 percent, ownership and land use about 15 percent, valuation about 14 percent, contracts about 12 percent, financing about 9 percent, transfer about 8 percent). The page says the exams still differ in emphasis and difficulty. Read the saved page rather than an older weight table from a prep site.
- Fees, from the fee page in the salesperson license folder: broker examination $150, original broker license $450, combined due to DRE with RE 436 is $600. Fingerprints only if DRE still needs a new set. On-time broker renewal on that page is $450. Late broker renewal within two years is $675.

Combined filing is RE 436, with instructions RE 436A. Exam-only is RE 400B. Re-examination is RE 415B. Prefer one filing, the same way as the salesperson application.

Members of the California State Bar are exempt from the college-level course requirements and still have to show experience. That exemption is not an engineering or MBA exemption.

This is not legal advice. Roadmap: [README](../README.md). Working checklist: [california-practice-path.md](../docs/california-practice-path.md).
